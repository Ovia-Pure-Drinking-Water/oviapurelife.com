ORDER QUANTITIES & DELIVERY

Clear minimums.
Clear expectations.

500 ml is supplied in packs of 12 bottles at Rs. 360 per pack. 1.5 Liter is supplied in packs of 6 bottles at Rs. 350 per pack. Individual-bottle orders are available for 6 L only.

500 ml

Karachi: minimum 50 packs for free delivery; below 50 packs, a delivery fee applies.

Out of city: minimum 500 packs; customer pays delivery.

Out of country: minimum 10,000 packs; customer pays delivery.

1.5 Liter

Karachi: minimum 60 packs with no delivery charge.

Out of city: minimum 600 packs with no delivery charge.

Out of country: minimum 12,000 packs with no delivery charge.

Save Rs. 1,000

Every 100 packs ordered earns Rs. 1,000 in savings, for either 500 ml or 1.5 Liter.

6 L pricing and delivery arrangements should be confirmed with Ovia.
